## §1 Charter

### 1.1 Mission

To decompose the mathematical continuum into physically real and physically vacuous components. The "uncountability of ℝ" is routinely invoked in the foundations of physics as a single fact — but it conflates three distinct properties with different ontological status. This trilogy separates them, eliminates what is physically vacuous, and identifies what is physically real.

### 1.2 Core Claim Lock

**Original Claim (informal):** "The uncountability of ℝ matters in physics."

**Reformulated (OC-operational):** The continuum as formalized in ZFC set theory (ℝ, with Dedekind completeness and power-set cardinality) contains two classes of structure:

1. **Depth (Archimedean completeness):** The connected, ordered completion of ℚ that permits limits, continuity, dynamics, and causality. Physically real.
2. **Breadth (set-theoretic uncountability):** The non-computable reals ℝ \ ℝ_c, generated by the power set of ℕ. Physically vacuous — pairwise unfalsifiable by any finite measurement protocol.
3. **Valuation (ultrametric completions):** The p-adic completions ℚ_p of ℚ for each prime p. Physically real — but their role is information-theoretic (encoding spin, color, and discrete quantum structure), not geometric.

**Core Thesis:** The physical continuum is ℝ_c × ∏_{p∈S} ℚ_p^c, where ℝ_c is the computable reals, ℚ_p^c the computable p-adic numbers at finitely many relevant primes S, and breadth (the dark continuum of non-computable reals) is eliminated entirely. Ostrowski's theorem is the organizing principle: there are no other completions of ℚ, so there are no other physically real dimensions of the continuum.

### 1.3 Scope

Three papers forming a single conceptual arc:
- **Paper I** establishes the Archimedean foundation (depth without breadth).
- **Paper II** establishes the p-adic dimension (valuation, spin, information).
- **Paper III** synthesizes into a unified adelic ontology.

### 1.4 Success Criteria

1. All three papers pass internal review (Publication Language Gate, Professional Publication Standards).
2. Each paper receives a Zenodo DOI.
3. Papers are deployed to papers.qnfo.org via D1 + papers-server Worker.
4. KG nodes created and connected for all three papers.
5. Social dissemination via Buffer on 3 channels.
6. Core Distribution Stack complete: GitHub + Zenodo + R2 + D1/KG.
