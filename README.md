Three papers decomposing the mathematical continuum into physically real and physically vacuous components.

## Reading Order

**Sequential** (recommended): I → II → III. Paper I establishes the Archimedean foundation (depth without breadth). Paper II introduces the p-adic dimension (valuation). Paper III synthesizes into a unified adelic ontology, decomposing the 2D depth/breadth model into the complete 3-axis framework.

**Modular**: Paper I is self-contained. Paper II references Paper I but can be read independently with background in p-adic analysis. Paper III assumes Papers I and II.
